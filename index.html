savidc-backup

savidc-backup is a private, personal tool. Its owner uses it to keep a read-only backup of their own Google Drive on their own home server.

It is not offered to, or used by, anyone else.

What it does

  • Uses read-only access to the owner's Google Drive. It never changes or deletes anything in Drive.
  • Every three months, saves a snapshot of the Drive to the owner's home server, keeping the two most recent snapshots.
  • Shares no data with anyone.
