savidc-backup privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

savidc-backup is a private, single-user backup tool. It is used only by its owner, with their own Google account, to keep a copy of their own Google Drive on their own home server. It is not offered to, or used by, anyone else.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's consent, the app uses the Google Drive API with the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly to list and download the owner's Google Drive files and their basic metadata (file names, folders, sizes and modification times). Google Docs, Sheets and Slides are downloaded as Microsoft Office files.

How the data is used

The data is used for one purpose only: creating a backup copy of the owner's Drive. The app never modifies, uploads to or deletes anything in Google Drive.

Where the data is stored and for how long

Backups are stored only on storage the owner controls (a home server on a private network). A new backup snapshot is created every three months, and only the two most recent snapshots are kept; older snapshots are deleted automatically.

Sharing

No data is collected by, sold to, shared with or transferred to any third party. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Security

The access token is stored only on the owner's home server. Data is transferred directly from Google over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backup copies can be deleted by the owner at any time from their home server.

Google API Services User Data Policy

savidc-backup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/avinasht1/savidc-backup/issues.
